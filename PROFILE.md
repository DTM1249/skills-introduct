## Paid App Idea: FocusRent

A subscription app that rents you a dedicated "focus room" — a minimal, distraction-free browser environment with a built-in Pomodoro timer, site blocker, and session notes. Users pay $8/month for unlimited sessions and cross-device sync. Unlike free alternatives, it enforces session commitments with a brief cooldown before you can unlock blocked sites, making accountability the core product.
